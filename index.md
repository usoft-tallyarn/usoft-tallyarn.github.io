# Tallyarn: Row Counter

Tallyarn is a simple row counter for knitting and crochet. Count rows with one big
tap zone, keep extra counters for pattern repeats, set reminders for specific rows
and keep notes for each project.

## Features

- **Big tap zone** for counting rows, with undo and an optional short vibration.
- **Extra counters** with their own step and target, up to six per project.
- **Linked counters and pattern repeats**: a repeat counter follows the row counter
  and starts over every N rows.
- **Row reminders**: one-off ("decrease on row 12") or repeating ("cable every 6th row").
- **Notes, pattern link and work timer** for every project.
- **Screen stays on** while you count.
- **Light and dark themes**, color themes and a left-hand mode.
- **No account and no sign-in.** Your projects are stored on your device only.

### Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Tallyarn works without an account and without signing in.

**Where are my projects stored?**
On your device only. We do not have a server and cannot see or restore your projects.

**What happens if I delete the app?**
Deleting the app deletes its data from the device. Export a backup file first if you
want to keep your projects.

**Why are there ads?**
Ads keep the app free. There are no full-screen ads while you are counting. You can
watch a short video in Settings to turn ads off for 24 hours.

**How do I change my ad privacy choices?**
Open Settings → Ads and privacy → Privacy choices.
