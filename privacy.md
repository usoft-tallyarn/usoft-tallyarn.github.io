# Privacy Policy for Tallyarn: Row Counter

**Effective date:** 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app **Tallyarn: Row Counter** ("Tallyarn",
"the app") handles information on Android and iOS. The app is developed by **Yevhen Shevchenko**
(FOP Shevchenko Yevhen Viktorovych / ФОП Шевченко Євген Вікторович), an
individual entrepreneur registered in Ukraine ("we", "us").

Contact: [usoft.tallyarn@gmail.com](mailto:usoft.tallyarn@gmail.com)

## Summary

- Tallyarn has **no account, no sign-in and no server of ours**. We do not collect,
  receive or store your projects or any other personal data.
- Everything you enter in the app stays **on your device**.
- The app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. The Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app
  collects some device data on its own to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  You control ad personalization through the consent form and the "Privacy choices"
  item in Settings.

## 1. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally, in the app's private storage on your device:

- **Projects:** names, craft type, counters and their values, steps and targets, linked
  counters, row reminders, notes, pattern links, work timer and the last counting actions
  (for undo).
- **Settings:** theme, color theme, left-hand mode, vibration and "keep screen on"
  choices, unlocked color themes.
- **Ad-related counters:** the end time of the "24 hours without ads" reward, the number
  of app sessions and the time of the last full-screen ad. They are used only on the
  device to follow the app's ad rules (for example, no full-screen ads in the first
  session).
- **Automatic snapshots:** once a day the app saves a copy of your projects and settings
  in its private data folder, keeping the three latest copies, so your data can be
  restored if the main storage is damaged.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it and cannot restore it
for you.

**Backup files.** When you choose to export a backup, the app creates a JSON file with
your projects and settings and opens the system share sheet. You decide where the file
goes (for example, a files app, a cloud drive or an email). The destination you choose
handles the file under its own terms. Importing a backup reads only the file you pick.

**System backups.** Your phone's operating system may include the app's data in device
backups (for example, Android backup to Google or iCloud backup), depending on your
device settings. Those backups are managed by Google or Apple, not by us.

**Pattern links.** When you open a pattern link, the page opens in an in-app browser
(Chrome Custom Tabs or Safari View). That website handles your visit under its own
privacy policy.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tallyarn shows banner, interstitial (full-screen, only during breaks, never while you
count) and rewarded ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland
Limited. To serve ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- **Device and advertising identifiers:** the Android Advertising ID or, on iOS, the
  Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) if you allow tracking, plus the identifier for vendors;
- **IP address,** which can be used to estimate your approximate location (country or
  city level);
- **App and ad interaction data:** app launches, ad views, taps and video completions;
- **Diagnostics:** crash and performance data related to the ads SDK;
- **Device information:** model, operating system and its version, language, screen size,
  network type.

Google uses this data to deliver and personalize ads (if you agree), measure ads,
calculate payments to publishers, and detect fraud and abuse. Data is encrypted in
transit. Google may process it in countries outside your own, including the United
States. Learn more:

- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google's list of ad technology providers](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903)

We do not receive the personal data collected by the SDK. We only see aggregated,
non-identifying reports (such as the number of ad impressions) in our AdMob account.

### Your choices about ads

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required).** Before ads are
  requested, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). You can agree,
  refuse or choose specific purposes. If you refuse, Google may still show
  non-personalized or limited ads.
- **Change your choice at any time** in the app: Settings → Ads and privacy →
  Privacy choices.
- **iOS tracking.** On iOS the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking
  Transparency). If you do not allow it, the IDFA is not shared. You can change this in
  iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Android advertising ID.** You can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy →
  Ads (or Google → Ads).
- **Ad-free time.** Watching a rewarded video in Settings turns ads off for 24 hours.

## 3. Permissions

The app uses only the permissions it needs:

- **Internet and network state:** to load ads. The rest of the app works offline.
- **Vibration:** for the short buzz on taps and reminders (you can turn it off in Settings).
- **Advertising ID (Android) / tracking request (iOS):** used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK
  as described above.

The app does not use your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, photos or push
notifications. Row reminders are shown inside the app on the counter screen.

## 4. Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your
message. We use them only to answer you and keep them only as long as needed to handle
your request. We do not add you to any mailing list.

## 5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data. The only third party that receives data
from the app is Google, through the ads SDK, as described in section 2.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- Project data stays on your device until you delete a project or uninstall the app.
  Uninstalling the app deletes its data and snapshots from the device (backup files you
  exported and system backups are kept where you stored them).
- Data collected by Google through the ads SDK is retained under
  [Google's retention policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can
still email us with any privacy question or request.

## 7. Children

Tallyarn is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal data to us by email, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU and UK GDPR, the Law of Ukraine
"On Personal Data Protection", or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may
have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict
its processing, and to withdraw consent. For data collected by the ads SDK you can use the
in-app "Privacy choices" item and the device settings above, or contact Google. For
anything else, write to [usoft.tallyarn@gmail.com](mailto:usoft.tallyarn@gmail.com).
You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## 9. Security

Your projects stay in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent
by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is 100%
secure, but we keep the app's data handling minimal on purpose.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The new version will be published
on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app
update description.
